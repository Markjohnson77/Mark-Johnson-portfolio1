Algomox – Product Experience Redesign

View Repository

Live Link Demo

Overview

Algomox is an enterprise AI-powered IT operations platform that helps organizations monitor infrastructure, automate operations, reduce downtime, and optimize operational efficiency through products like ITMox, Nora, and Algomox.Cloud.

The Problem

The existing website communicated Algomox’s offerings well, but the product experience felt text-heavy, visually fragmented, and lacked strong product storytelling. Core products like ITMox, Nora, and Algomox.Cloud were not immediately understandable to first-time visitors.

The Solution

A modern product-led landing page redesign that clearly showcases Algomox’s ecosystem through interactive dashboard previews, AI assistant interfaces, platform visualizations, and modular content sections.

Key Features

  • Hero Section with product-first storytelling
  • ITMox Dashboard Preview with KPI cards
  • Nora AI Assistant Interface
  • Algomox.Cloud Control Plane Visualization
  • Interactive Product Modules & Animated Counters
  • Dark / Light Mode & Responsive Design

Design Approach

Focused on dashboard-first storytelling, enterprise-grade visual hierarchy, clean spacing, modular card-based layouts, and Algomox-inspired blue/cyan branding. Designed to make complex IT products easy to understand at first glance.

Impact & Learnings

Improved product clarity, stronger visual storytelling, and a more modern SaaS experience. Key learning was understanding enterprise product ecosystems and designing interfaces that simplify complex operational workflows.

FULL CASE STUDY

CollegeGO – Smart Bus Pass & Movement Approval System

Overview

CollegeGO is a web-based platform that digitizes student movement requests and bus pass approvals through a structured multi-level workflow. It replaces manual paperwork with a secure, role-based approval system.

The Problem

Bus pass approvals in colleges are often handled manually, leading to delayed approvals, lack of transparency, and administrative overload. Students often face uncertainty regarding their request status while administrators manage physical forms.

The Solution

A digital approval workflow: Student → HOD → Warden. Providing real-time tracking, status-based dashboards, and paperless record management.

My Role

  • UI/UX Design
  • Frontend Development
  • Backend Integration
  • Database Design

Key Features

  • Role-based Access

  • Real-time Tracking
  • Responsive UI
